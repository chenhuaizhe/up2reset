Support

Most questions are in the FAQ. If you are stuck, email is faster than a review.

Find the app

Up2Reset runs in the menu bar, not the Dock. After launch, look near the top-right of the screen for the timer.

Restore Pro

Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that paid.

Report a bug

Email chenhuaizhe@gmail.com with:

  • macOS version
  • Up2Reset / Up2Stretch version (Settings, or About)
  • What you did, and what happened
  • One extra monitor or not, if the overlay is involved

You can also use the App Store listing contact, but email gets a usable thread.
